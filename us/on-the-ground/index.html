Local support when you arrive

A strong guide helps you make informed decisions. Local support helps you put them into practice: see places as residents do, coordinate the right conversations, and handle the practical details that are hard to solve from abroad.

Ewa and her team

Ewa is the founder and head of the team behind Portugal Next. She and her team of Locals and Expats speak English and German and bring first-hand knowledge of everyday life in the Algarve. They work alongside independent lawyers, real estate agents, interior designers and accountants, connecting clients with the right expertise when a decision needs professional advice.

What local guidance can help with

Before you choose

Area and lifestyle orientation

Compare neighbourhoods and towns against your actual routine: services, transport, seasonality, community and housing style.

Property search

On-the-ground context

Arrange visits, explain practical trade-offs and coordinate introductions. Your independent lawyer and technical inspector should verify legal and building matters.

Settling in

Arrival coordination

Help organise practical appointments, introductions and local services so you are not trying to coordinate every detail from another time zone.

Specialist decisions

Trusted professionals

Connect with appropriate lawyers, accountants, real estate agents and interior designers for their own specialist advice and services.

Clear roles make decisions safer

Local orientation is not legal, tax, immigration, financial or medical advice. The relevant licensed professional must assess your own documents and circumstances, set their own scope and fees, and remain responsible for their advice. You choose whom to engage and make the final decisions.

Make the first conversation useful

Bring your move window, likely visa route, household needs, housing priorities, healthcare considerations and the questions you have not been able to answer remotely. You do not need to have every decision made; the goal is to identify what is known, what needs verifying and what a sensible next step looks like.

Prepared for your move: the topic guides and AI prompt library help you arrive with a clearer brief. Local support then focuses on the parts that benefit from seeing, meeting and coordinating in person.

What to send Ewa before a scouting visit

Two addresses and a description of a normal week give us more to work with than a list of favorite towns. For example: “We are comparing a flat in Lagos with a house above Praia da Luz. One of us will work Eastern time. We have one car and would like to walk to a grocery store. We can visit Monday and Tuesday.” Add your rental budget, who will be coming and what would rule a home out.

Use that brief to plan a morning of errands, the journey to school or work, and a second visit to the preferred area in the evening. Keep a short list of decisions after the visit. The local team can help identify where to get an answer. A lawyer should review a lease clause, a surveyor can investigate damp, and the school admissions office can confirm a place.
