Residence visas and first steps

A visa is a route with eligibility rules, evidence, timing and follow-up steps. Start from your purpose and circumstances; verify the current checklist with the Portuguese authority or consulate handling your application.

Visiting before you move

US citizens can generally visit the Schengen area for up to 90 days in any rolling 180-day period without a short-stay visa. That allowance does not itself authorise a long-term residence or local work. Count days across the whole Schengen area, not Portugal alone.

Common routes to investigate

Independent means

D7 / own-income route

For people whose qualifying income and circumstances fit Portugal’s residence visa for retirees or people living on their own income. Verify what income is accepted and how dependants are assessed.

Remote work

Remote-work route

For qualifying remote employees or service providers working for entities outside Portugal. Requirements include evidence of the working relationship and income; do not assume a US employer’s permission alone is enough.

Portuguese employment

Work residence visa

For a qualifying job or employment arrangement in Portugal. The employer, contract and applicable labour/immigration process matter.

Family / other cases

Family reunification and other routes

Family relationships, study, investment-related eligibility, entrepreneurship and other circumstances have separate rules. Get advice on your exact basis; buying a home is not a general residence visa.

Build the application file early

  • Confirm the consulate or visa centre responsible for your place of residence and use its current checklist.
  • Check passport validity and obtain civil-status records, police certificates, apostilles and translations as required.
  • Document the route-specific basis: income, remote-work arrangement, employment contract, family relationship or business plan.
  • Arrange accommodation evidence, insurance, financial evidence and any required criminal-record documents in the form requested.
  • Keep a dated copy of every submitted item, receipt, appointment and official communication.

From arrival to a residence permit

Visa issuance and the residence permit are related but distinct steps. Read the visa conditions, attend the required appointment and retain proof of submission. AIMA’s current process and appointment availability can change. Once resident, keep your address and contact details current and check renewal dates well in advance.

Planning trap: do not set a non-refundable property completion, job start or US tax-residence end date based on an assumed visa-processing timeline. Build contingencies into contracts and travel.

Useful questions for your adviser

  • Which exact residence basis fits my facts, and which family members can apply with me?
  • What evidence, apostille and translation requirements apply to my jurisdiction?
  • Can I work for my US employer or clients under this route, and what Portuguese registrations follow?
  • What is the expected sequence from application to residence permit, and what can delay it?

Two households, two different files

Consider a retired couple living on Social Security and a pension. For an own-income application, put each award letter beside the statements showing the corresponding payments. Note whose name is on each document, the payment frequency and whether either income ends on the death of a spouse. Ask the consular office how it wants to see the second spouse’s means and relationship documented.

Now consider a software designer keeping a US job. The same bank balance tells the examiner little about where the work takes place. The useful file includes the employment agreement, a letter authorizing work from Portugal, recent evidence of earnings and the details requested for the remote-work route. The employer still needs to resolve its own payroll and social-security questions. See working from Portugal.

Time the documents around the appointment

Start a sheet with one row per person and document. Record the issuer, who can order it, whether an apostille or translation is requested, when it expires for this application, and where the original will be on the appointment date. A marriage certificate can be shared evidence across two files, while each person’s passport or background check needs its own row. Use the checklist supplied for your route and US jurisdiction to fill in the dates. Save the version you worked from.

If an appointment moves, check short-lived evidence first. If the visa is issued, read the actual entry window, permitted entries and residence appointment instructions on your own documents before booking onward travel. Portugal visa applications from the US
