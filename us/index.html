Moving to Portugal from the United States

If you are considering moving from the US to the Algarve, three decisions shape almost everything else: what income you will live on, which town suits an ordinary week, and when you can realistically move.

Where to start

  • If you are retiring, start with the household budget. Compare it with your after-tax pension income, then check the own-income residence route and how you will cover healthcare from the day you arrive.
  • If you are keeping a US job, begin with the employer questions. Resolve permission, payroll and work location before agreeing to a lease or a start date.
  • If you are moving with children, shortlist schools first. A place in the right year group and a school run you can manage will narrow your choice of towns.

Planning a first visit

Take two real listings at roughly the same rent, one in each town you are considering. Stay nearby on a weekday. Make the journey to the supermarket, a health center and whichever school, station or workplace you expect to use. In the evening, try the route home again. Those observations will tell you whether a cheaper home would mean a second car or a longer school day.
