Driving and getting around the Algarve

Can you live in the Algarve without a car? An address within walking distance of Faro station and a hillside house above Praia da Luz give very different answers. Test the journeys you make most often before choosing the house.

Read the rail map from your front door

CP’s regional railway runs between Lagos, Faro and Vila Real de Santo António. Stations include Portimão, Albufeira-Ferreiras, Loulé, Olhão and Tavira. Look up the station itself on a map. In particular, searching for Albufeira town center can hide the extra leg to Albufeira-Ferreiras. Check your actual train, any change at Faro, and the last service back on the day you intend to travel.

Praia da Luz illustrates a different trade-off. A Onda’s yellow bus line serves Luz and Lagos. Pick the stop closest to the property and walk to it with a bag of shopping. Check the return timetable on a Sunday as well as a weekday. Someone who likes walking downhill to the beach may feel differently about the uphill trip home from the bus.

Getting home from Faro Airport

The airport operator lists buses from the terminal into Faro. To continue by rail, budget time to reach the city station and buy your onward ticket. Compare that journey with a direct coach or a prebooked transfer to your town. A late flight, two suitcases and a child can change which option makes sense.

For visits to the US, price the whole itinerary in the month you plan to travel. Compare Faro with a connection against reaching Lisbon separately. If flights are on separate tickets, check what each airline will do after a delay. Include the trip home after your return flight, parking for the length of your stay, and a hotel if the last train departs too early.

Using a US driver’s license

Portugal provides conditional recognition for qualifying licenses from OECD countries, including the United States. Among the conditions are a valid license, a holder under 60, and no more than 15 years since issue or latest renewal. The driver must meet the Portuguese minimum age for the category, and the license must remain in good standing. The rules concern driving in Portugal. Check other countries separately if you plan to drive into Spain.

If you are approaching 60, your card does not show the original issue history, or your license expires soon, take your dates and license classes to IMT, Portugal’s transport authority. Ask whether and when you must exchange it, which proof your US state should issue, and what you can drive while the application is pending. It is easier to request a driving record while you still have access to your state DMV account.

Price the weeks when you really need a car

For one shortlisted address, write down a grocery run, a routine appointment, a night out and an airport trip. Price those by bus, rail or taxi, including the return. Compare the monthly total with insurance, parking, fuel, maintenance and purchase or lease costs for a car. If both adults need the car at the same time, include what the second person will do.
