Renting or buying a home

A home purchase is not a residence permit. First establish your legal route and budget, then inspect the property, paperwork, access and realistic running costs with independent professionals.

Rent first if you still need to learn the area

A short holiday stay rarely reveals winter damp, traffic, noise, parking, road conditions, service availability or how far everyday errands really are. If practical, rent for a season before choosing a permanent home. Read termination, deposit, utilities and maintenance provisions carefully.

Buying: a practical due-diligence sequence

  1. Set a total budget including taxes, legal fees, technical inspections, furniture and immediate repairs.
  2. Use an independent Portuguese lawyer to verify title, encumbrances, boundaries, licensing/use, condominium status and contract terms.
  3. Commission an appropriate technical survey; inspect roof, drainage, damp, wiring, plumbing, pool systems, access and outbuildings.
  4. Confirm whether the property’s registered and licensed use matches your intended use; do not rely only on marketing descriptions.
  5. Review the promissory contract (CPCV), conditions, deposit, completion date, remedies and what happens if financing or documents are delayed.
  6. Before closing, obtain written calculations for IMT, stamp duty, notary/registration and legal costs from your lawyer/accountant.

Budget for ownership after closing

Ask for current IMI and whether AIMI may apply to your assets; check condominium charges, insurance, utilities, waste/water arrangements, maintenance and any planned building works. Tax bases, rates, exemptions and thresholds change. Confirm the figures for this property and tax year before signing.

Questions for a viewing

  • What is the legal access route, and is it registered?
  • Are all extensions, pools, terraces and outbuildings authorised and registered?
  • What are the actual winter utility bills and the condition of insulation/heating?
  • Is the property in a condominium, protected area, flood/fire exposure zone or subject to easements?
  • What maintenance is due soon, and who has inspected it independently?
  • Are rental assumptions lawful and realistic if you expect income?
Do not send a deposit based on urgency alone. Have the documents reviewed, the recipient verified and the agreed conditions written into the contract before funds move.
