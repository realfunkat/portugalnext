Your US-to-Portugal moving checklist

Use this as a working sequence, not a promise that every step takes a fixed number of weeks. Consular appointments, residence processing, document validity and your own circumstances can shift the timing.

12–9 months before

  • Define the purpose of the move, likely route, family members and target arrival window.
  • Take a scouting trip in ordinary-season conditions; compare at least two or three locations.
  • Engage US and Portuguese tax professionals to model the residence date, income, accounts and assets.
  • Ask your employer, clients or business advisers for written approval and compliance analysis.
  • Review healthcare, prescriptions and insurance continuation; identify specialist records to obtain.

9–6 months before

  • Confirm the current visa checklist with the responsible Portuguese consular office.
  • Request police/civil certificates and track their validity periods; order apostilles and translations as instructed.
  • Build complete income, accommodation, insurance and family evidence.
  • Make a US account and asset inventory; discuss FBAR/Form 8938 and other filings with your preparer.
  • Decide whether to rent first; avoid binding property assumptions until immigration and tax timelines are clear.

3–1 months before

  • Confirm visa status, travel permissions, insurance dates, accommodation and contingency plans.
  • Request medical summaries and medication supply planning; carry prescriptions in original packaging.
  • Plan mail, banking access, two-factor authentication, US state ties, phone service and document backups.
  • Obtain written moving/transport quotes and clarify customs treatment for household goods, vehicle and pets.
  • Make copies of passport, visa, civil records, apostilles, contracts, insurance and emergency contacts.

First 30–90 days after arrival

  • Follow the visa/residence instructions and keep appointment and submission confirmations.
  • Complete tax-number, address, bank and healthcare steps that apply to your case.
  • Confirm work and social-security registrations with the relevant professionals.
  • Track every day in Portugal, the US and other countries for tax and travel records.
  • Update US advisers with actual arrival dates and any changed income, employment or asset plans.
  • Set reminders for residence renewal, insurance renewal and tax filing deadlines in both countries.

Travel arrangements for pets and drivers

For pets, start well before travel: microchip, rabies vaccination and the required official veterinary documents depend on origin and timing. For driving, verify the current rules for your license type, length of residence and any conditions that apply to US licenses; do not rely only on the rental-car counter.

What goes in the suitcase and what goes in the shipment?

Keep originals needed for entry and early appointments with you. Include the lease, insurance evidence, prescriptions and medical summary, and any school or pet records you need on arrival. If partners travel separately, decide who carries shared civil records. Keep secure copies accessible if a phone or bag goes missing.

Give two movers the same inventory and both street addresses. Ask each to itemize packing, insurance, stairs or lift access, delivery from port to door, storage charges and responsibility for customs paperwork. Check the practical details for the Algarve address. A narrow old-town street or an apartment without a lift can change the delivery quote even if the ocean freight price stays the same.

What depends on what in the first week

  • Before changing your number, test US banking sign-in and account recovery. Make sure a second authorized person can reach funds if your phone is lost.
  • When you receive the keys, record meter readings, inventory, mailbox access and where official letters will arrive. Confirm your full postal address with the owner.
  • Once the address is certain, identify your health center, school group and any service needing proof of residence. Check which accept a lease and which request additional evidence.
  • If a key date slips, review the end of temporary housing, insurance, school start, mover storage and any pet-certificate window. Put the cost of an extra week beside each booking.
