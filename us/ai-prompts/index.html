A considered starting point

Use AI as a research assistant and checklist builder, not as your immigration lawyer, tax preparer, accountant, doctor or property inspector. These prompts are designed to make the tool explain its reasoning and show where the answer may fail.

Protect your information. Do not paste passport numbers, tax IDs, account statements, medical records, exact home addresses or unredacted contracts into a general AI tool. Replace personal details with placeholders and verify every legal, tax or healthcare conclusion with an appropriately qualified professional.

Prompts you can copy

Replace the bracketed fields. Ask for dated official sources and confirm that the AI has not silently assumed your visa, tax status or family situation.

1 · Compare residence routes

I am a US citizen currently living in [US state/country]. I plan to move to Portugal around [month/year]. My situation: [retired / remote employee / self-employed / seeking local work / other], household members [general details], income sources [categories only]. Compare the Portuguese residence routes that may fit. For each, list eligibility questions, official documents, which authority sets the current checklist, what you cannot conclude, and the exact official sources with page dates. Do not infer that buying property grants residence. End with questions for a Portuguese immigration lawyer.

2 · Build my tax adviser brief

Help me prepare a fact inventory for a US–Portugal cross-border tax adviser. My move window is [dates]; I am a US citizen and expect these income categories: [categories, not amounts unless needed]. I have these account/entity types: [IRA, 401(k), bank, brokerage, LLC, trust, etc.]. Make a two-country checklist covering residence dates, federal/state US returns, Portuguese filings, foreign tax credit/exclusion questions, FBAR, Form 8938, pensions and planned asset sales. Separate confirmed rules from questions requiring advice. Cite current IRS, FinCEN and Portuguese Tax Authority sources. Do not calculate my tax liability.

3 · Check remote-work compliance questions

I will live in Portugal and work remotely for [a US employer / my own business / foreign clients]. Without deciding my legal status, create a checklist of issues to confirm with immigration counsel, my employer and tax/social-security advisers: visa permission, employer location approval, payroll, Portuguese registration, social security, permanent establishment, VAT, US benefits and data security. Cite current official Portuguese sources and label uncertainties.

4 · Plan healthcare continuity

I expect to move from the US to Portugal in [month/year]. Give me a non-medical administrative checklist for continuity of care: when my current cover may end, Medicare limits abroad, transition insurance questions, medical records, prescriptions, SNS registration and private policy comparisons. Do not recommend stopping or changing treatment. Use official Medicare and Portuguese SNS sources, identify what depends on my exact plan, and give questions for my physician and insurer.

5 · Review a property decision

I am considering renting/buying in [general Algarve area]. Here is a redacted description of the property: [details]. Create a due-diligence question list for an independent Portuguese lawyer, technical inspector and accountant. Include title, permits, boundaries/access, damp/roof/drainage, utilities, condominium, IMT/stamp duty/IMI, contract conditions and ongoing costs. Do not tell me the property is legally sound or calculate taxes without verified facts. Cite official Portuguese sources where possible.

6 · Compare Algarve areas

Compare [Lagos, Portimão, Faro, Loulé, Tavira, other] for my ordinary week: [healthcare, airport, public transport, driving, year-round services, quiet, community, budget range]. Separate verified facts from subjective impressions. Use exact travel distances only if sourced and dated. Identify what I should test on a winter visit, and do not rank an area without explaining the trade-offs.

7 · Turn official instructions into a checklist

Using only these official pages/documents that I provide: [paste public URLs or non-sensitive text], extract every requirement into a checklist. For each item, quote only a short phrase, explain who provides it, whether it needs apostille/translation, any validity period stated, and the source section/page. Mark missing or conflicting information rather than guessing. Tell me when the page was accessed.

8 · Make a 12-month move plan

Create a 12-month move plan for a US citizen moving to Portugal in [month/year]. My likely route is [route, if confirmed], work situation [category], household [general], and key needs [healthcare/school/pet/property]. Sequence tasks, mark dependencies and suggested lead times, but label timing as planning estimates. Include source links for legal requirements and a separate list of decisions that need licensed professionals. Do not assume my visa is approved.

9 · Challenge an answer

Audit your previous answer for errors. List every assumption, identify claims that may have changed since the cited source, replace secondary sources with official sources where possible, and show the exact text that supports each key claim. Give the strongest counterargument to your recommendation. If you cannot verify a claim, say so clearly instead of filling the gap.

How to use the answers responsibly

  1. Ask for the answer date and official sources.
  2. Open the source and check that it actually says what the AI claims.
  3. Keep the AI output as a draft checklist, not as proof of eligibility or advice.
  4. Take your fact pattern and questions to the relevant qualified professional.
  5. Repeat the check shortly before acting, because rules and procedures can change.
