Healthcare, insurance and Medicare

Plan how to maintain cover before, during and after the move, including the limits of Medicare abroad and how to register locally.

A considered starting point

The best healthcare plan is one you can use during the transition, not just after paperwork is complete. Map your medications, doctors, insurance, emergency cover and chronic-care needs before leaving the US.

Do not assume Medicare travels with you

Original Medicare generally does not cover health care outside the United States, with limited exceptions. Medicare Advantage and Medigap rules differ; check your exact plan and network. Moving abroad may also affect enrollment choices and late-enrollment penalties if you later return without qualifying coverage.

Bridge the gap

Ask your US insurer what happens on the date you become a non-resident or leave the service area. For a visa application, check what insurance the consulate requires, when it must start, exclusions, deductibles, pre-existing conditions and repatriation. Keep proof of cover and plan how prescriptions will be continued or replaced.

Using Portugal’s SNS

Foreign residents may register with the National Health Service (SNS) after establishing eligibility and obtaining a user number. Official guidance lists documents such as a Portuguese tax number, local address and residence documentation for foreign nationals. Registration at a local health centre and access routes can depend on your situation and local capacity.

Prepare a personal continuity file

  • Medical summary, current diagnoses, allergies, surgeries and vaccination record.
  • Generic and brand names, dosage, quantity and prescriber for every medication.
  • Copies of recent test results and specialist reports; translate essential records if needed.
  • Written confirmation of when US coverage ends and what international coverage starts.
  • Emergency contacts, preferred hospital, and plan for urgent and routine appointments.
  • Ask in advance which medicines require Portuguese prescriptions or have different rules.

Private cover and public care can serve different needs

Private insurance may offer different provider choice or appointment access, but policy terms, exclusions, co-payments and renewal conditions matter. SNS registration does not mean every service is immediate or that private policies are unnecessary for your own circumstances. Compare actual coverage rather than relying on a brand name or sales summary.

For ongoing treatment: speak with your physician and insurer before moving. Confirm the next appointment, medication supply and the receiving care pathway; do not interrupt prescribed treatment based on general relocation information.
