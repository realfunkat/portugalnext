Bank accounts, transfers and your NIF

Rent is due in euros while your pension or paycheck may still arrive in dollars. Before departure, make sure you can open the account you need, move money into it and approve payments from a Portuguese phone.

Get your Portuguese tax number

The Número de Identificação Fiscal (NIF) identifies you to the Portuguese tax authority, often called Finanças. You will come across it when opening a bank account and signing contracts. The tax authority publishes separate application instructions for residents and nonresidents. If you are applying from the US, use the route that matches your address at the time of application.

For a nonresident application, start with your passport and proof of your foreign address. Read the authority’s instructions for your circumstances, including what a representative must supply if someone applies for you. Check the spelling of your name and the address on the confirmation. If you later become tax resident, follow Finanças’ instructions to update the record. An NIF issued while you lived abroad does not by itself establish a residence date.

Before the bank appointment

Ask the bank whether it opens the account you want for US citizens at your current address. Explain if you are retired, employed or self-employed and whether one or both partners will hold the account. Ask which originals or digital documents it accepts and how recent the proof of address must be. This is especially useful if the utility bill is in only one partner’s name.

  • Identity and tax details. Have your passport, NIF confirmation and requested US taxpayer details ready. Use the bank’s secure submission channel.
  • Address and occupation. Ask whether it accepts a bank statement, utility bill, employer letter or pension statement for your circumstances.
  • Access. Confirm where the debit card and PIN will be delivered, which number receives approval codes and how both holders can log in.

Compare the cost of moving the same dollars

Ask two providers, at roughly the same time, how many euros will actually reach your account for a $5,000 transfer. Include the sending fee, exchange-rate spread and any receiving fee. For example, if the final amounts are €4,500 and €4,440, the difference is €60 even if both providers advertise a free transfer. Separately compare the bank’s monthly maintenance fee, debit card charge and transfer limits.

Make one payment before rent is due

Find your IBAN, the account identifier used for a bank transfer. Confirm the landlord’s payment details against the signed lease and verify any change by calling a number you already trust. Test a small transfer between your own accounts, download the receipt, then check the amount and arrival date at the other end. Ask your US bank whether it will keep servicing your account at a Portuguese address, and test its sign-in recovery before changing phone numbers.

Save the account opening date, joint holders, account details and statements for your US preparer. A temporarily large balance while you move house can matter for reporting. The US–Portugal tax chapter explains how foreign-account reporting works.
