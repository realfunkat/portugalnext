The cost of living in the Algarve

How far will your US income go in the Algarve? Start with two addresses where you would actually live. The difference in rent between towns can outweigh everything you save on groceries or eating out.

What rent buys in three Algarve towns

In October 2026, Idealista reported the following advertised rents per square meter across three municipalities. The final column applies each figure to the same 80 m² home (about 860 sq ft). It gives you a starting point for comparing towns. The apartment you find may cost more or less, particularly if you need a furnished, year-round lease.

On this basis, Lagos comes out €760 a month above Faro, or €9,120 over a year. Check three current listings in each town. Ask whether the quoted rent covers July and August, whether utilities are included, and whether you can renew. A low winter price is little help if you need to move out in June.

The same spending with the Lagos rent example comes to €3,290 a month. Children, private schooling, an ongoing US mortgage, car finance or regular specialist care would change either figure substantially. Compare spending with the income you expect to receive after US and Portuguese taxes.

How much cash will you need to arrive?

Draw a line between money you spend and money you tie up. If your signed lease calls for €1,200 for the first month and a €1,200 refundable deposit, you need €2,400 on hand at signing. Count the first month once in your annual budget. Keep the deposit in a separate column so you can see how much cash remains available after the move.

Put flights, temporary accommodation, movers, document fees and any overlap with your US home on a calendar. Give each a payment date. If you receive dollars, test the exchange rate too. At hypothetical rates of $1.10 and $1.20 per euro, the same €2,530 month costs $2,783 or $3,036 before transfer fees. Use the rate and fees your provider quotes when you move money.
